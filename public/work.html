How it works

One organization, every department, a league that runs itself.

Create your organization

Spin up your agency or company in the app and share one join link — in Teams, on a break-room poster, anywhere. Work-email domains verify colleagues automatically.

2

Departments field crews

Parks vs Public Works. Engineering vs Sales. Each department runs its own weekly games and calls out another with one tap — both teams get the challenge.

3

Standings & reports write themselves

The Department Cup, the Activity Cup and the player board update from confirmed scores. Every month, a participation report builds itself for leadership — one tap to share.
