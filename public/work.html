How it works

From “we should play sometime” to a standing run.

1

Make your crew

Create a private crew for your office and share one link — in Slack, on a poster, anywhere. Colleagues land in the crew even before they have the app.

2

Set the weekly run

Pick the day, time and court once. Everyone RSVPs in one tap, week after week, and guests get a calendar invite that actually shows up.

3

Play. The rest is automatic.

Score it live from the court (or your wrist). Results post to the crew, standings and streaks update themselves, and “run it back” is one tap.

Built for the office run

Everything the spreadsheet did, and everything it couldn’t.

🔒

Private by default

Workplace crews and their games are invite-only and off the public map. Your company run stays your company’s.

⚡

Crew vs crew

Call out another team — engineering vs sales, office vs office. Teams sort themselves by crew and results count toward the rivalry.

📊

Standings that write themselves

Wins, losses, streaks and player ratings update from confirmed scores. Nobody keys results into a sheet on Monday.

💬

One thread, everything in it

The crew chat carries the next run, RSVPs, photos and final scores — pinned at the top so nobody scrolls to find the plan.

🎯

Crew goals

Set a games-per-month target and watch the crew chase it. A little shared accountability keeps the run alive through busy weeks.

📺

Live scoreboard

Courtside scorekeeping with an Apple Watch companion — spectating colleagues follow along in real time.
