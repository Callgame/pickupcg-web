Our Product

Pickup: Call Game

Find a game near you, host your own weekly run, and keep your crew coming back — basketball, pickleball, soccer, tennis, flag football and more. Live on the App Store, with recurring games, guest RSVPs that need no account, live scoreboards, and an Apple Watch companion.

What We Care About

Reliability first

A game that says it's on, is on. Automatic confirmations mean nobody drives to an empty court.

Privacy by default

Location sharing is opt-in and coarse by default. No ads, no data sales — in our products, you are the customer, not the product.

Real-world community

Software is the means, not the end. Success is measured in people showing up to play, week after week.

About

Call Game LLC is a Florida limited liability company headquartered in Weston, Florida. We design, build, and operate consumer software for local sports communities.

Our flagship product, Pickup, launched on the App Store in 2026 and supports twelve-plus sports with game discovery, recurring runs, crews, ladders, and live scoring across iPhone and Apple Watch, with Android on the way.
