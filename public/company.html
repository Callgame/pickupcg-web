The Principles

How we play.

Play No. 1Reliability first

A game that says it's on, is on. Automatic confirmations mean nobody drives to an empty court.

Play No. 2Privacy by default

Location sharing is opt-in and coarse by default, and games and crews can be fully private — invite-only, off the public map. No ads, no data sales.

Play No. 3Real-world community

Software is the means, not the end. Success is measured in people showing up to play, week after week.

Pickup for Teams & Workplaces

Another way to connect that isn't happy hour.

Organizations use Pickup as a workplace-culture resource: a private crew, one join link, and a standing weekly run — an inclusive, alcohol-free way for colleagues to actually spend time together. We run structured pilot programs for companies and public agencies. Ask us at info@callgame.co.

The Franchise

About Call Game LLC.

Call Game LLC is a Florida limited liability company headquartered in Weston, Florida. We design, build, and operate consumer software for local sports communities.

Our flagship product, Pickup, launched in 2026 and supports twelve-plus sports with game discovery, recurring runs, private crews, ladders, and live scoring across iPhone, Android, and Apple Watch.
