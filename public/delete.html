Delete your Pickup account

This page explains how to delete your Pickup account (published by Call Game LLC), what data is removed, and the small amount we are required to keep.

Delete from inside the app — takes about 30 seconds
  1. Open Pickup and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab.
  3. Scroll to Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

Don't have the app installed?

Email info@callgame.co from the email address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Pickup account.” We verify the request and delete the account within 30 days, usually within 1–2 business days.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your profile — name, username, photo, bio, city, skill level, date of birth
  • Your games — games you hosted or joined, rosters, scores, and results
  • Your posts, photos, videos, and clips
  • Your messages — direct messages, crew chats, and game chats
  • Your social graph — follows, friends, crews, invites, and challenges
  • Your location data and any saved courts
  • Your notification tokens and app-activity analytics identifiers

What we keep, and why

Two narrow exceptions exist so that deleting an account can't be used to erase a safety record or evade a ban:

  • Safety reports you filed about other people are kept, but your identity is removed from them — the report becomes anonymous. Reports filed about you are also kept. Without this, anyone could erase evidence of harmful behaviour by deleting their account.
  • A one-way hashed version of your email address is kept only if your account was suspended, or had reports filed against it. It cannot be reversed to recover your email; it exists solely to stop a suspended account from being recreated. If your account was in good standing, nothing is kept.

Retention period

  • Immediately: your account and the data listed above are deleted from our live systems as soon as you confirm.
  • Within 30 days: residual copies in encrypted database backups are purged as backups rotate.
  • Indefinitely: only the anonymised safety reports and, where applicable, the hashed identifier described above.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

You don't have to delete your whole account to remove individual content. In the app you can delete your own posts, clips, and messages, leave crews and games, and turn off location sharing in Me → Settings.
