How it works

From install to game
in under 2 minutes.

1
Download and pick your sports
Choose the sports you play. Pickup immediately shows you every open game nearby, sorted by distance and start time. Set your skill level once — games filter to match automatically.
2
Browse the live map
See a pin for every game near you. Tap any pin for full details — sport, host, skill level, who's already in, and spots remaining.
3
Join a game or post your own
Tap Join and you're in — spots update in real time. Or tap + to post your own game, fill the sport-specific fields, and watch your spots fill.
4
Show up, play, and log the result
Get a push notification before tip-off. Check in at the court. After the game, post the score — it counts toward your record and the local leaderboard.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Pickup free?
Yes. Finding, joining, and posting games is always free — no subscription and no paywall. Pickup earns through venue partnerships, not player fees.
What sports can I play?
12+ and counting — basketball, pickleball, tennis, soccer, volleyball, flag football, running, cycling, padel, badminton, and more. Each sport has its own fields and filters.
Where is Pickup available?
Pickup is live now across South Florida and expanding. Anyone with an iPhone can download it today and start posting games in their area.
Do I need a team or a full roster?
No. Pickup is built for open games — join solo, fill your own game one player at a time, and see the skill level before you commit.
Is there an Android version?
Pickup is on iPhone and iPad today. Android is on the way — join the waitlist and we'll let you know the moment it lands.
How do I post a game?
Tap the + button, pick your sport, set the court, time, and skill level, and post. Your game appears on the live map instantly and players can join in one tap.
