How it works

Your courts, your staff, your players. We handle the app.

1

We set you up

Sign the free pilot agreement. We verify your venue and make your account its owner. You invite your staff from the app; each posts from their own account.

2

Post pop-up games

Open runs, doubles nights, drop-in clinics. Set the spots and a minimum. Players nearby find it in the app, and your followers get a push.

3

Run league nights

A season of 6 to 8 weekly nights. Players sign up once and say each week if they're in. Teams are made on the night, and the app keeps the table.

Pop-up games

Everything a front desk needs, nothing it has to chase.

✓

Hosted by your venue

Pop-ups your staff post as your venue say “Hosted by” it with a check mark, so players know it's the real thing. Only venues we've verified get it.

🔔

Players follow you

Your venue page has a Follow button, a share link and directions. Followers get a push when you post: at most one a day from your venue, never overnight, and only if they have new-game alerts on.

📱

Front-desk check-in

Show your check-in code on a screen at the desk. Players scan it with their phone's camera to check in, or join and check in at once. The code changes every day.

⏱

A minimum and a cut-off

Set the fewest players a game needs and a cut-off 1, 2 or 3 hours before. If it's short by then, it's called off, and everyone signed up or on the waitlist hears.

↻

Run it again

A game that worked? Post it again a week later in one tap, with the same time, spots, minimum and cut-off.

📊

Your venue, in numbers

Your followers, plus pop-ups posted, desk check-ins and first-time check-ins over the last 30 days. Counts only, never a list of players' names.

League nights

An in-house league without the clipboard.

Pick a night, a time and how many players. Pickup posts every night of the season, announces it to your followers and league members, and keeps the table from desk check-ins and the results your staff record.

  • Come when you can. Players sign up once per season and tap “I'm in” each week. “Can't make it” frees the seat for the next person.
  • Teams on the night. Sides are made from whoever's there, so nobody needs a full team to join.
  • Showing up counts. 1 point for showing up, 1 for a win. Missing a week costs nothing, and ties go to whoever played more nights.
  • No prices in the app. Pickup never shows players a price. If you require a membership, turn on “Membership required” and players see it before they join.
  • Kept in the venue. Only league members and your staff see the standings.
  • A league chat. One group chat for everyone in the league.
  • Keep me in. Once you post the next season, one tap signs a player up again.
  • Your staff run it. Record each night's winners, call off a night ahead of time and post the next season, all in the app.
