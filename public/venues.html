How it works

Your courts, your staff, your price. We handle the app.

1

We set you up

Sign the free pilot agreement. We verify your venue and make your account its owner. You invite your staff from the app; each posts from their own account.

2

Post pop-up games

Open runs, doubles nights, drop-in clinics. Set the spots, the fee and a minimum. Your followers hear about it, and players nearby find it in the app.

3

Run league nights

A season of 6 to 8 weekly nights. Players sign up once and say each week if they're in. Teams are made on the night, and the standings keep themselves.

Pop-up games

Everything a front desk needs, nothing it has to chase.

✓

Hosted by your venue

Every game your staff post says “Hosted by” your venue with a check mark, so players know it's the real thing. Only venues we've verified get it.

🔔

Players follow you

Your venue page has directions, a share link and a Follow button. Followers hear when you post, never more than twice a day.

📱

Front-desk check-in

Show your check-in code on a screen at the desk. Players scan it with their phone's camera to check in, or join and check in at once. The code changes every day.

⏱

A minimum and a cut-off

Set the fewest players a game needs and a cut-off 1, 2 or 3 hours before. If it's short by then, it's called off and everyone signed up hears.

↻

Run it again

A game that worked? Post it again for the next week in one tap, with the same time, spots, fee and cut-off.

📊

Your venue, in numbers

Your followers, plus pop-ups posted, desk check-ins and first-time visitors over the last 30 days. Counts only, never a list of players' names.

League nights

An in-house league without the clipboard.

Pick a night, a time, how many players and the fee. Pickup posts every night of the season, tells your followers and league members, and keeps the table.

  • Come when you can. Players sign up once per season and tap “I'm in” each week. “Can't make it” frees the seat for the next person.
  • Teams on the night. Sides are made from whoever's there, so nobody needs a full team to join.
  • Showing up counts. 1 point for showing up, 1 for a win. Missing a week costs nothing, and ties go to whoever played more nights.
  • Paid at your desk. Set a fee per night. Players pay you when they come, as they do today. There are no payments in the app.
  • Members only. Only people in the league see its standings.
  • A league chat. One group chat for everyone in the league.
  • Keep me in. When a season wraps, one tap carries a player into the next.
  • Your staff run it. Record each night's winners, call off a night and start the next season, all in the app.
